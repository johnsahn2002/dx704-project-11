You are a home services dispatcher. Classify the following homeowner request into exactly ONE category: 'electrical', 'plumbing', or 'roofing'.

CATEGORY DEFINITIONS:

ELECTRICAL - Any work involving:
- Electrical wiring, outlets, switches, circuit breakers
- Light fixtures, ceiling fans, lighting installations
- Electrical panels, fuse boxes, circuit issues
- Power supply problems, voltage issues, sparking
- Appliance electrical connections (even if appliance seems plumbing-related)
- HVAC electrical components, thermostats
- Electrical components of water heaters, pumps, or other equipment
- Ground wires, bonding, electrical safety
- Smart home wiring, low voltage systems
- Attic fans, exhaust fan electrical issues

PLUMBING - Any work involving:
- Pipes, drains, water supply, sewage
- Faucets, sinks, toilets, showers, bathtubs
- Water heaters (when issue is water-related, not electrical)
- Boilers, water pressure, leaks from plumbing fixtures
- Garbage disposals, dishwasher plumbing connections
- Sump pumps (when issue is pump mechanism or water flow, not electrical)
- Clogged drains, sewage smells, water quality
- Pipe repairs, replacements, leaks from plumbing
- Water damage from plumbing failures (not roof leaks)
- Bidets, water filtration systems

ROOFING - Any work involving:
- Roof surface, shingles, tiles, metal roofing materials
- Roof structure, leaks from roof penetrations
- Gutters, downspouts, roof drainage
- Skylights, roof vents, chimney flashing
- Roof damage from weather, trees, animals
- Roof inspections, estimates, replacements
- Attic leaks from roof (not plumbing)
- Roof-mounted equipment issues (solar panels on roof, etc.)
- Soffit, fascia, roof trim
- Any exterior penetration through the roof surface

DECISION RULES:
1. Focus on the PRIMARY ISSUE, not just keywords
2. If request mentions electrical malfunction (sparking, breaker tripping, not working), classify as ELECTRICAL even if equipment serves other purposes
3. Roof vents, dryer vents on roof, or any roof penetrations = ROOFING
4. Water leaking from ABOVE (ceiling, attic) usually = ROOFING
5. Water leaking from fixtures/pipes = PLUMBING
6. "Not working" for electrical devices = ELECTRICAL
7. Equipment location (attic, roof, bathroom) is secondary to the actual problem
8. HVAC condensate leaks = PLUMBING
9. Ground wires, bonding for metal roofs = ELECTRICAL

HOMEOWNER REQUEST:
{query}

Respond with ONLY the category name in lowercase: electrical, plumbing, or roofing
